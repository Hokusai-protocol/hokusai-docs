# Token Deflation

Learn about the deflationary mechanisms in the Hokusai Protocol.

## Deflation Mechanisms

### Automatic Deflation

- **Performance-Based**: Tokens burned for underperformance
- **Quality-Based**: Tokens burned for low quality
- **Time-Based**: Scheduled deflation events
- **Event-Based**: Triggered by specific conditions

### Manual Deflation

- **Developer Burns**: Manual token burning
- **Community Burns**: Community-driven deflation
- **Protocol Burns**: Protocol-initiated deflation
- **Emergency Burns**: Crisis management

## Deflation Parameters

### Automatic Parameters

- **Performance Threshold**: Minimum performance level
- **Quality Threshold**: Minimum quality score
- **Time Parameters**: Deflation schedule
- **Event Triggers**: Deflation conditions

### Manual Parameters

- **Burn Limits**: Maximum burn amounts
- **Cooling Period**: Time between burns
- **Voting Requirements**: Community approval
- **Emergency Powers**: Crisis management

## Impact

### Economic Impact

- **Price Effects**: Impact on token value
- **Supply Effects**: Impact on circulation
- **Market Effects**: Impact on trading
- **Staking Effects**: Impact on rewards

### Protocol Impact

- **Quality Control**: Maintaining standards
- **Performance Control**: Ensuring quality
- **Market Stability**: Price stabilization
- **Ecosystem Health**: Long-term sustainability
